## Tasarım yönü
DIRECTION: 'AHBU Quiet Premium' - the company's own design language (G:/site/gudeteknolojiweb/docs/DESIGN_SPEC.md and THEMING.md, lib/theme.dart) applied to a control panel: graphite-black surfaces (#06080B / #11151B), ONE cool turquoise accent (#58D6CB; #0E9F94 / #0B766D on light), hairline borders, wide soft shadows, Manrope, spring physics. This matches the plan's 'calm, reliable, alive - a home panel used in the dark' (docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-01-denetim-duzeltmeleri.md section 5c). The circuit identity survives only as a faint hairline trace texture (<= 6% alpha, token-driven) and the logo badge; the neon JPGs go.

DELIBERATE DECISIONS NEEDING OWNER SIGN-OFF: (a) physical light is the only warm colour (lamp glow in the house hero and the bulb glyph) - the site spec says 'no gold/peach in UI', so this is an exception; fallback = accent-coloured glow. (b) retiring neon imagery. (c) two test edits listed in mustNotBreak. (d) optional removal of emoji from pinned titles (needs 3 test strings changed).

SIGNATURE: the house is the interface - an animated cross-section whose rooms glow with real lamp state and whose shutters sit at real positions, readable in under 2 seconds (TASKS 5.2) and doubling as the room filter. Supporting cast: tactile surface cards, themed Material Switch with haptics and pending halo, motor-honest linear shutter travel with spring settle, staggered 'scene wave' when scenarios run, skeletons instead of spinners, calm status halo, one-tap Hepsini Kapat reminder banner.

RULES: (1) animate on change, rest at idle - no infinite loops; ambient effects only while visible + foreground + tier full + MotionScope(ambient:true) (so bare-MaterialApp tests are static and pumpAndSettle can never hang).
